Privacy Policy: Classroom Pull

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Classroom Pull is a private, personal-use tool built and operated by an individual developer. It is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner, to view the owner's own Google Classroom information.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the app reads the owner's Google Classroom courses, announcements, course materials, topics and coursework, along with the Google Drive files that teachers attach to those posts, such as weekly homework PDFs. The app performs read operations only and does not create, edit or delete anything in Google Classroom or Google Drive, although Google's consent screen may describe the coursework permission as including edit access.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to give the owner summaries and plans for the owner's own children's schoolwork. It is stored in the owner's private file storage. To produce those summaries, the text of messages and files may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) acting on the owner's behalf, subject to that service's own terms and privacy policy. The app itself does not sell or share the data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data can be deleted by deleting the stored files.
